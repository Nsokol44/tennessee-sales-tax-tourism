# Source notes: TDTD county visitor spending

## Source document

- Title: 2025 Economic Impact of Travel on Tennessee
- Publisher: Tennessee Department of Tourist Development (TDTD), prepared by Tourism Economics. Source line in the report: "Sources: USTA, Tourism Economics".
- Edition: calendar-year 2025. It replaces the calendar-year 2024 edition (statewide total $31.66B).
- URL: https://industry.tnvacation.com/sites/default/files/2026-09/2025%20Economic%20Impact%20of%20Travel%20on%20Tennessee%20%281%29.pdf (linked from https://industry.tnvacation.com/industry/research)
- Local copy name: `2025 Economic Impact of Travel on Tennessee (1).pdf`, 62 PDF pages. The file is not in the repository; `fetch_data.py` downloads it.

## Where the county figures are

- Alphabetical county-level data: report pages 14-37 (PDF pages 16-39). Each county block gives 2024 and 2025 values for every metric. This table is extracted into `tdtd_county_visitor_spending.csv`.
- County rankings by direct visitor spending: report pages 12-13.
- Statewide totals: report page 11.
- Methodology and glossary: report page 56. Notes on use of the data: report page 51.

## Metric

The county blocks label the headline row SPENDING. The report calls the metric direct visitor spending, and states that all figures are direct impacts only. County figures cover domestic and international visitors and exclude indirect and induced effects. In the CSV the column is `visitor_spending_usd`.

## Columns

Each county has one row per year (2024, 2025).

| CSV column | Printed row label |
|---|---|
| `visitor_spending_usd` | SPENDING |
| `spending_lodging_usd` | LODGING |
| `spending_food_beverages_usd` | FOOD & BEVERAGES |
| `spending_retail_usd` | RETAIL |
| `spending_recreation_usd` | RECREATION |
| `spending_transportation_usd` | TRANSPORTATION |
| `labor_income_usd` | LABOR INCOME |
| `employment_jobs` | EMPLOYMENT |
| `state_tax_receipts_usd` | STATE TAXES |
| `local_tax_receipts_usd` | LOCAL TAXES |
| `tax_savings_per_household_usd` | TAX SAVINGS PER HOUSEHOLD |
| `source_report_page` | printed page where the county block appears |

From the report glossary: labor income is wages, salaries, proprietor income and benefits supported by visitor spending; employment follows the Bureau of Economic Analysis definition (full- and part-time jobs); state and local taxes are tax revenues generated by visitor spending.

## Checks on the extraction

1. Coverage: all 95 counties are present, giving 190 rows (95 counties, 2 years).
2. Statewide totals:
   - 2024: the 95 county values sum to $31,662,137,675; the printed statewide total (page 11) is $31,662,137,672 (difference $3).
   - 2025: the county values sum to $32,527,758,355; the printed total is $32,527,758,351 (difference $4).
   The differences are rounding in the report.
3. Components: for every county and year, lodging, food and beverages, retail, recreation and transportation sum to the SPENDING total within $5.
4. Rankings table: the 2025 spending for each county matches the value in the rankings table (pages 12-13) for all 95 counties.
5. Knox County 2025 values (spending $2,208,345,965; state taxes $139,671,017; local taxes $81,648,077) match the figures published by Visit Knoxville from the same study.

`extraction_script.py` performs checks 2 to 4. It reads per-page text made with `pdftotext -layout` (see the script header for usage).

## Notes from the report

- The report says its figures will not match reports from before 2020, and that some 2024 county figures differ from earlier publications because of improved data; history was revised back to 2018. Example: Sevier County 2024 spending is $3,964,231,200 here and was $3,929,693,370 in the 2024 edition. The 2024 column of this edition is used throughout.
- Page 51 describes the county figures as modeled estimates designed to be comparable across all 95 counties, and says local studies may give different numbers for individual counties.
- The 2025 international spending share is preliminary; the 2024 domestic and international split is final.
- Suggested citation: Tennessee Department of Tourist Development (2026). 2025 Economic Impact of Travel on Tennessee. Nashville, TN: TDTD; prepared by Tourism Economics.

No value in the CSV was estimated, interpolated or taken from a secondary source.
